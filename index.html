Responsible AI
Risk identification and mitigation — embedded in every AI output
⚖️
Algorithmic Bias
Risk: CRITICAL
AI triage may over- or under-prioritise incidents based on how they're described. Language quality and cultural phrasing can skew urgency scores unpredictably.
All AI outputs include a confidence disclaimer. Human coordinator must confirm before any resource deployment. Triage is advisory — never directive.
🧑‍💼
Over-Reliance
Risk: CRITICAL
Under stress, coordinators may defer entirely to AI. This creates a single point of failure if the model hallucinates or receives incomplete data during a real emergency.
AI never auto-dispatches. Every analysis requires human confirmation via "Confirm & Add". Training materials enforce human-in-loop at all times.
🔒
Data Privacy
Risk: HIGH
Incident reports may contain personal information. Data sent to AI APIs passes through third-party infrastructure outside the operator's direct control.
No personal identifiers included in AI prompts. Role-based anonymisation applied. All data in this prototype is synthetic and simulated.
🌐
Language Barriers
Risk: MEDIUM
Currently English-only. In multilingual regions, volunteers may submit inaccurate descriptions, degrading AI output at the worst possible moment.
Arabic support on the v3 roadmap. AI prompts handle fragmented or non-native English. Bilingual data entry guides provided.
📡
Connectivity Dependency
Risk: MEDIUM
AI analysis requires active internet to the Claude API. Connectivity may degrade exactly when AI assistance is most critical during a major incident.
Core queue and reporting work offline. AI is enhancement, not load-bearing infrastructure. Paper-based fallback triage protocol provided.
📊
Accountability & Audit
Risk: LOW
AI-assisted emergency decisions must be auditable. If a recommendation contributed to harm, a traceable record of the analysis and human decision is required.
All analyses timestamped in Session Log. Field report captures outcomes. Future version will hash AI outputs for tamper-proof audit logs.
